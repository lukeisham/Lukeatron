* **Situational Judgement Tests (SJTs):** Scenario-based problems that evaluate ethics, risk prioritization, integrity, and split-second decision-making under competing or high-stress priorities.
* Test examples:
  * **US:** Casper (Acuity Insights)
  * **UK:** UCAT - Situational Judgement Test Subtest
  * **AU:** Casper, UCAT ANZ - Situational Judgement Test Subtest

* **Verbal Critical Reasoning:** Dense text passages followed by statements where you must evaluate argument validity, identify unstated assumptions, detect logical fallacies, or determine if an inference is logically supported.
* Test examples:
  * **US:** MCAT - Critical Analysis and Reasoning Skills (CARS), Watson-Glaser Critical Thinking Appraisal
  * **UK:** UCAT - Verbal Reasoning Subtest, LNAT (National Admissions Test for Law)
  * **AU:** GAMSAT - Section 1 (Reasoning in Humanities and Social Sciences), UCAT ANZ - Verbal Reasoning Subtest

* **Abstract / Diagrammatic / Fluid Reasoning:** Non-verbal matrix and pattern-completion problems that test visual pattern recognition, sequence discovery, and rule extraction without relying on language.
* Test examples:
  * **US:** Raven's Progressive Matrices (RPM)
  * **UK:** SHL Diagrammatic / Inductive Reasoning Test
  * **AU:** ACER Vocational/Selection Aptitude Tests (Abstract Reasoning components)

* **Deductive & Analytical Reasoning:** Formal logic problems involving syllogisms, logic grids, constraint-satisfaction puzzles, and evaluating whether conditions are necessary or sufficient.
* Test examples:
  * **US:** LSAT - Logical Reasoning, GMAT Focus Edition - Critical Reasoning *(Note: LSAT Analytical Reasoning / Logic Games removed August 2024)*
  * **UK:** UCAT - Decision Making Subtest, TSA (Thinking Skills Assessment - Critical Thinking elements)
  * **AU:** UCAT ANZ - Decision Making Subtest

* **Data Interpretation & Quantitative Reasoning:** Complex charts, statistical tables, or raw data sets where candidates must extract relevant signal from irrelevant noise and perform multi-step analysis under tight time limits.
* Test examples: 
  * **US:** MCAT - Chemical & Physical Foundations / Data-Based Statistical Reasoning, GRE - Quantitative Reasoning
  * **UK:** UCAT - Quantitative Reasoning Subtest
  * **AU:** UCAT ANZ - Quantitative Reasoning Subtest, GAMSAT - Section 3 (Reasoning in Biological and Physical Sciences), STAT Multiple Choice (Quantitative Reasoning portion)
