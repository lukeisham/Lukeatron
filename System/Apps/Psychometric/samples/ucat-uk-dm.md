## UCAT DM — Syllogism: Yes/No Conclusions

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Format:** Drag-and-drop Yes/No — evaluate each conclusion against the text passage.

### Stimulus

All patients on Ward 7 who tested positive for the respiratory virus have been moved to isolation rooms. Some patients in isolation rooms are receiving antiviral medication. No patient in an isolation room is permitted to have visitors.

### Question

For each of the following conclusions, indicate whether it **follows** (Yes) or **does not follow** (No) from the information provided.

### Conclusions

- **A:** All patients on Ward 7 are in isolation rooms.
- **B:** Some patients receiving antiviral medication are not permitted to have visitors.
- **C:** No patient on Ward 7 who tested positive for the respiratory virus is permitted to have visitors.
- **D:** All patients receiving antiviral medication are in isolation rooms.
- **E:** Some patients on Ward 7 are receiving antiviral medication.

### Correct Answer Key

| Conclusion | Follows? | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | No | "All patients on Ward 7 who tested positive" is a subset; we don't know about patients on Ward 7 who tested negative. They may not be in isolation rooms. |
| **B** | Yes | "Some patients in isolation rooms are receiving antiviral medication" + "No patient in an isolation room is permitted to have visitors." The overlap of isolation + medication exists, and all of them are subject to the no-visitors rule. |
| **C** | Yes | All positive-tested patients are in isolation rooms (premise 1); no patient in isolation rooms has visitors (premise 3). Syllogism: All A are B; no B are C; therefore no A are C. Follows. |
| **D** | No | The premises say "some patients in isolation rooms are receiving antiviral medication." This does not imply the reverse: there may be patients receiving antiviral medication who are *not* in isolation rooms (e.g., outpatients, patients on other wards). "Some A are B" does not entail "all B are A." |
| **E** | No | We know all positive patients are in isolation, and some isolation patients receive antivirals. But we cannot determine whether any of the positive patients are among those receiving antivirals. The overlap may exist entirely among non-positive patients in isolation rooms. |

### Clue

Draw a Venn diagram: a circle for "Ward 7 positive patients" entirely inside "isolation rooms"; a "antiviral medication" circle overlapping partially with "isolation rooms"; "visitors" completely outside "isolation rooms." Then check each conclusion.

### Explainer

UCAT DM syllogisms test precise logical reasoning under time pressure. The key skill is resisting the urge to make plausible-sounding inferences that are not logically forced by the premises. Common traps: (1) inferring "all" from "some" (D); (2) inferring overlap where premises only establish one-directional containment (E); (3) forgetting that "all A are B" doesn't mean "all B are A" (A). Partial Venn diagrams drawn quickly on the provided whiteboard are the recommended strategy.
