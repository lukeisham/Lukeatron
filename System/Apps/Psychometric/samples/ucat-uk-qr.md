# UCAT — Quantitative Reasoning Subtest — UK

**Reference test:** UCAT — Quantitative Reasoning subtest (UK)
**Format:** 36 questions, 24 minutes. Presented in sets of 4 questions sharing a common data stimulus (table, chart, or graph). On-screen calculator available. Time-pressured (~40 seconds per question).
**Answer style:** Single correct answer (A–E). Numeric tolerance ±5%.
**Distinctive feature:** UCAT QR uses real-world data contexts. Questions often require extracting relevant information from dense tables with distractors (irrelevant rows/columns), converting between units, and applying percentage changes or ratios.

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Context:** A data set used for 4 questions.

### Questions

**1.** What is the annual council tax charge for a single-person household living in a Band D property in Millford?

- **A:** £1,407.82
- **B:** £1,877.09
- **C:** £1,332.78
- **D:** £1,476.14
- **E:** £1,968.60

**Correct Answer:** **A (£1,407.82)**

**Explanation:** Millford, Band D = £1,877.09. Single-person discount: 25% off → charge = £1,877.09 × 0.75 = £1,407.82 (rounded to the nearest penny).

---

**2.** A couple living in a Band A property in Oakbury move to a Band C property in Crayford. By how much does their annual council tax increase?

- **A:** £394.88
- **B:** £437.44
- **C:** £565.36
- **D:** £627.92
- **E:** £749.88

**Correct Answer:** **C (£565.36)**

**Explanation:** Original charge: Oakbury, Band A = £1,184.52. New charge: Crayford, Band C = £1,749.88. Increase = £1,749.88 − £1,184.52 = £565.36.

---

**3.** How much more council tax does a Band H property in Crayford pay compared to a Band H property in Oakbury, per year?

- **A:** £200.49
- **B:** £383.54
- **C:** £446.68
- **D:** £683.54
- **E:** £937.18

**Correct Answer:** **B (£383.54)**

**Explanation:** Crayford H = £3,937.18; Oakbury H = £3,553.64. Difference = £3,937.18 − £3,553.64 = £383.54.

---

**4.** A single person living in a Band E property in Crayford claims their 25% single-person discount. The Crayford parish bylaw states the discount does not apply to Band H only. What is their discounted annual charge?

- **A:** £1,804.55
- **B:** £2,406.06
- **C:** £1,628.72
- **D:** £1,684.24
- **E:** Cannot be determined from the information given.

**Correct Answer:** **A (£1,804.55)**

**Explanation:** Crayford, Band E = £2,406.06. The bylaw restricts the discount only for Band H, not Band E. Therefore the 25% discount applies: £2,406.06 × 0.75 = £1,804.55 (rounded).

### Explainer

UCAT Quantitative Reasoning tests the ability to navigate dense data tables under time pressure. Key skills: (1) locating the correct row and column quickly — distractors are rows and columns you don't need; (2) applying percentage changes (discounts, increases) correctly; (3) reading footnotes and special conditions (the Crayford bylaw); (4) avoiding simple arithmetic errors under time pressure. The on-screen calculator should be used for verification, but mental estimation can eliminate obviously wrong answers quickly. For Q1: £1,877 × 0.75 — estimate as slightly less than £1,877 − £470 = ~£1,407.
