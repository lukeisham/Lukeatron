# Function

- Clarity — making meaning accessible
  - Clarity — makes an idea easier to understand
    - Comparison — explains the unfamiliar by likening it to the familiar
    - Definition & Gloss — pins down or explains a term or phrase as it is used
    - Ordering — lays out the parts and their sequence so the audience can follow
  - Conciseness — says the point in as few words as it will bear
  - Concrete Grounding — anchors an abstract point in something specific and tangible
    - Vivid Description — sets a scene, person or sound before the senses
    - Embodiment — gives an abstraction or non-human thing a body, voice or character
    - Example — grounds a point in a specific case or instance
- Emphasis — drawing attention, making memorable
  - Emphasis — puts extra weight on a point
    - Repetition — presses a point by saying a word, sound or structure again
    - Accumulation — piles up words, questions or restatements on one point
    - Escalation — builds a series that rises in force
    - Distortion of Scale — makes a thing stand out by overstating or understating it
    - Balance & Contrast — lets the shape of the sentence carry the weight
    - Interruption & Turn — breaks off, retracts, caps or changes voice for effect
  - Memorability — makes a point easy to recall
  - Foregrounding — pulls one element to the front of the audience's attention
- Persuasion — convincing the audience
  - Logical Appeal — persuades by reasoning and evidence
    - Deduction — the conclusion is meant to follow from the premises by form
    - Induction & Evidence — reasons from cases, samples or data to a general claim
    - Cause & Consequence — reasons about what causes what, or what will follow
    - Standards & Values — reasons about rules, categories, norms and oughts
    - The Source — judges a claim by who makes it or where it came from
    - The Exchange — handles the opponent's argument within the debate
  - Emotional Appeal — persuades by stirring feeling
    - Sympathy — moves the audience to pity, tenderness or longing
    - Indignation & Contempt — stirs anger, scorn or disgust
    - Fear & Hope — moves by what is promised or threatened
    - Colouring & Display — shades feeling through word choice, exclamation or show
  - Authority/Power — persuades by standing, credentials or force
    - Expertise — rests a claim on someone who knows the subject
    - Precedent & the Many — rests a claim on the past, tradition or the crowd
    - Command & Force — imposes by law, threat or flat refusal to engage
- Aesthetic — creating beauty, pleasure, style
  - Aesthetic Effect — gives the language beauty or polish for its own sake
    - Sound & Symmetry — pleases the ear through matched sounds and balanced shapes
    - Ornamental Naming — names or describes a thing with poetic polish
  - Wit & Humor — delights or amuses through cleverness
    - Wordplay — plays on a word's double sense, sound or reach
    - Repartee — turns an exchange with a quick, clever reply
  - Elegance — achieves its effect with grace and economy
- Connection — building rapport, communion
  - Shared Understanding — draws speaker and audience onto common ground
  - Intimacy — creates closeness between speaker and audience
- Disruption — breaking expectation, jarring
  - Surprise — upsets what the audience was expecting
  - Irony — says or shows one thing while meaning another
- Evasion — avoiding, deflecting or softening what would otherwise be said
