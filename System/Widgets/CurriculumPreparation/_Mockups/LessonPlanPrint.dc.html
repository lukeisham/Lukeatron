Curriculum
[CODE] The commemoration of World War I and contested debates about the Anzac legend

Key example
The 1990 "Return to Gallipoli" broadcast, next to a 1965 newspaper editorial.

What changed between these two accounts — the events, or the audience?
Assessment
Final · Was the Anzac legend made in 1915? Mini · Source reliability drill
