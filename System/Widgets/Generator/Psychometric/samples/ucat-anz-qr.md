## UCAT ANZ QR Stimulus — GP Bulk Billing Rates

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Context:** Australian healthcare. 4 questions sharing one data set.

### Stimulus

**TABLE: Bulk Billing Rates by Medicare Region — Quarterly Data, Q2 2024**

| Medicare Region | Total GP Consultations | Bulk Billed (%) | Average Gap Fee (non-bulk billed) | Patient Concession Card Holders (%) |
|-----------------|------------------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Western Sydney | 84,200 | 82.5% | $38.50 | 34% |
| South-East Melbourne | 67,500 | 71.2% | $42.00 | 28% |
| Brisbane North | 58,300 | 76.8% | $39.80 | 31% |
| Adelaide Central | 42,100 | 85.1% | $35.20 | 36% |
| Perth Coastal | 49,800 | 68.4% | $44.60 | 23% |
| Hobart & Southern Tas | 21,600 | 88.3% | $32.10 | 41% |

*Note: Bulk billing means the GP accepts the Medicare rebate as full payment, so the patient pays nothing. Non-bulk billed patients pay the full fee upfront and receive a Medicare rebate of $41.20 per standard consultation. The "gap fee" is the amount the patient pays out of pocket above the rebate.*

---

### Questions

**1.** In South-East Melbourne, approximately how many GP consultations in Q2 2024 were NOT bulk billed?

- **A:** 15,840
- **B:** 19,440
- **C:** 23,760
- **D:** 28,080
- **E:** 48,060

**Correct Answer:** **B (19,440)**

**Explanation:** Non-bulk billed % = 100% − 71.2% = 28.8%. 67,500 × 0.288 = 19,440 consultations.

---

**2.** A non-bulk billed patient attending a standard GP consultation in Perth Coastal pays the average gap fee. What total fee does the patient pay upfront (before receiving the Medicare rebate)?

- **A:** $41.20
- **B:** $44.60
- **C:** $68.40
- **D:** $85.80
- **E:** $127.00

**Correct Answer:** **D ($85.80)**

**Explanation:** Total upfront fee = Medicare rebate + gap fee = $41.20 + $44.60 = $85.80.

---

**3.** In Brisbane North, what total revenue was collected from gap fees for non-bulk billed consultations? Assume each non-bulk billed consultation attracts the average gap fee.

- **A:** $257,390
- **B:** $538,320
- **C:** $580,400
- **D:** $623,420
- **E:** $923,400

**Correct Answer:** **B ($538,320)**

**Explanation:** Non-bulk billed consultations = 58,300 × (100% − 76.8%) = 58,300 × 0.232 = 13,525.6. Revenue = 13,525.6 × $39.80 = $538,318.88, rounded to $538,320. Within the ±5% tolerance, B is correct.

---

**4.** If the federal government introduced a policy requiring all Medicare regions where the bulk billing rate is below 75% to increase their rate by 5 percentage points within 12 months, how many regions in the table would be affected?

- **A:** 1
- **B:** 2
- **C:** 3
- **D:** 4
- **E:** 5

**Correct Answer:** **B (2)**

**Explanation:** Regions below 75% bulk billing:
- South-East Melbourne: 71.2% — affected
- Perth Coastal: 68.4% — affected
- All others (Western Sydney 82.5%, Brisbane North 76.8%, Adelaide Central 85.1%, Hobart 88.3%) are at or above 75%. So 2 regions.

### Explainer

UCAT ANZ Quantitative Reasoning tests numerical reasoning with Australian-relevant contexts (Medicare, bulk billing, gap fees). Key skills tested: (1) calculating proportions of populations (Q1, Q3); (2) understanding fee structures with multiple components (Q2 — total fee = rebate + gap); (3) working with revenue calculations (Q3); (4) applying threshold-based policy rules to data (Q4). The on-screen calculator should be used for precise calculations, but quick estimation can eliminate distractors — e.g., for Q4, scanning the "Bulk Billed %" column takes 5 seconds.
